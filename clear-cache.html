🔄 Clear Browser Cache

If the WordPress Support Hub is not working correctly, clear your browser cache to force reload the updated files.

🚀 Quick Fix Methods:

  1. Hard Refresh: Press Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
  2. DevTools Method: Open DevTools (F12) → Right-click refresh → "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"
